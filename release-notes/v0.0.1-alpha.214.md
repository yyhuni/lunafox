# v0.0.1-alpha.214

## English

### Added

- Global asset search accepts the words and and or between field conditions. The word and means every condition must match, the same as &&. The word or means any condition may match, the same as ||.

Full Changelog: https://github.com/yyhuni/lunafox/compare/v0.0.1-alpha.213...v0.0.1-alpha.214

## 简体中文

### 新增

- 全局资产搜索接受字段条件之间的单词 and 和 or。and 表示每个条件都必须满足，与 && 相同。or 表示满足任意一个条件即可，与 || 相同。
